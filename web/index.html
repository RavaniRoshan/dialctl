Privacy & scope

100% Local Browser Execution. Uploaded CSVs never leave this device. They are parsed in-browser and stored only in your local IndexedDB (trai_a2p_audit_v1). Use Clear stored data to wipe all local audit data and preferences at any time.

Worst-Case Undeclared / Non-Exempt A2P Termination Exposure is an estimate at ₹0.05 per ceiling minute (Tata Communications TRAI Filing). It is an upper bound for undeclared, non-exempt A2P traffic — not a bill, quote, or guarantee.
